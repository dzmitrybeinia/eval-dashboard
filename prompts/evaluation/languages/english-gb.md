# British English Language-Specific Instructions

## LANGUAGE FOCUS: British English (US Contamination Detection)

Evaluate US English contamination in British English content. Flag only patterns that are verifiable present in the text; do not infer or assume US terms exist.

**US PATTERNS TO DETECT:**

1. **Spelling**: -or endings instead of -our, -er endings instead of -re, -yze endings instead of -yse, -ize endings instead of -ise*, single consonants before suffixes where British doubles them, -ense endings instead of -ence
[*Note: -ize is acceptable in Oxford spelling but check consistency throughout the document]

2. **Core vocabulary**: US terms where British English uses different words entirely, particularly in domains of transport, housing, clothing, public infrastructure, seasons, academic subjects, family terms, and sports

3. **Grammar & usage**:
- Past tense: US past participle and past tense forms that differ from British conventions
- Prepositions: US prepositional patterns where British English uses different constructions
- Phrases: Colloquial expressions characteristic of US English

4. **Dates**: Month-first format (US) → Day-first format (UK)

5. **Punctuation**: Full stops after honorifics and abbreviations (US) → No full stops (UK)

6. **Currency/Units**: Use £ and metric for UK context (not $ or US customary units)

**DO NOT FLAG:**
- Proper nouns and brand names
- Technical terms with standardised spellings (code syntax, API parameters)
- Direct quotes in US English
- US-specific concepts with no British equivalent
- International standards
- Foreign currency when contextually appropriate
